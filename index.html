Premier Water Infrastructure & Automation Solutions Across India

Orbit Engineering Solutions (formerly Orbit Engineering Group), based in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, is India's leading engineering company specializing in Water Treatment Plants (WTP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), Reverse Osmosis (RO), Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), SCADA systems, PLC automation panels, IoT water monitoring, and process instrumentation since 1998.

With ₹200+ Crore delivered project portfolio and Triple ISO Certification (ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018), we are a trusted partner for MP Jal Nigam, MPUDCL, Bhopal Municipal Corporation, and leading industrial clients nationwide.

Our Core Capabilities

  • Water & Wastewater Treatment: Turnkey WTP, STP, Industrial RO Plants, ETP, and advanced filtration systems.
  • Automation & SCADA: Industrial PLC panels, SCADA software integration, RTU stations, and IoT telemetry for pumping stations and water grids.
  • Process Instrumentation: Electromagnetic flow meters, ultrasonic & hydrostatic level transmitters, differential pressure sensors, and online water analyzers (pH, Turbidity, DO, BOD, COD, Chlorine).
  • Valves & Electric Actuators: Multi-turn and part-turn electric actuators, butterfly valves, gate valves, and motorized control valves.
  • Specialty Water Tech: Submersible Dredging Vehicles (SDV) and Real-Time Scour Detection systems for bridge foundations.
